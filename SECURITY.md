# Security Policy

Thank you for helping keep TOK and its users secure.

We take security seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure of potential vulnerabilities.

---

## Supported Versions

Only the latest production version of TOK receives security updates.

| Version | Supported |
| ------- | :-------: |
| Latest  | ✅ |
| Older versions | ❌ |

Please ensure you are using the latest version before reporting an issue.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**

Instead, contact us privately at:

**security@thetok.ch**

Please include:

- A detailed description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Any proof of concept or screenshots
- The potential impact
- Any suggested remediation (optional)

If the report is valid, we will investigate it as quickly as possible.

---

## Response Timeline

Our typical response process is:

- **Within 48 hours:** Acknowledgement of your report.
- **Within 7 days:** Initial assessment and status update.
- **As soon as possible:** Development and deployment of a fix, depending on severity.
- **After resolution:** We may publicly acknowledge your contribution (with your permission).

---

## Responsible Disclosure

We ask that you:

- Give us a reasonable amount of time to investigate and resolve the issue before making it public.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data that does not belong to you.
- Do not perform actions that could impact the availability or integrity of our services.
- Limit testing to what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.

We will not pursue legal action against security researchers who act in good faith and follow this policy.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- The TOK web application
- Official APIs
- Official repositories owned by the TOK organization
- Services hosted under the **thetok.ch** domain

Third-party services and dependencies should be reported directly to their respective vendors.

---

## Security Updates

Security fixes are released as soon as reasonably possible based on the severity of the issue.

We strongly recommend always using the latest version of TOK.

---

Thank you for helping us keep TOK secure.
